# BONEPILE — Visual Asset Manifest

## Proyecto
Bonepile — Protocolos de Supervivencia SMT

Este documento consolida todas las referencias visuales, personajes, escenas y conceptos generados durante la etapa conceptual del videojuego.

---

# 1. MAIN CHARACTER — R3-W0RK

## Descripción General
Unidad SOIC-16 rework survivor.
Personaje principal del juego.
Diseño inspirado en:

- Componentes SMT
- Robots industriales retrofuturistas
- estética PS1 / N64
- maquinaria rework
- cyberpunk industrial

## Características

- cuerpo principal tipo SOIC-16
- patas mecánicas hechas de terminales electrónicos
- brazo soldering iron
- brazo clamp/gripper
- cables expuestos
- capa rasgada
- desgaste industrial
- iluminación tenue verde/ámbar

## Prompt Maestro

```text
Ultra detailed 3D render of a small industrial robot creature made from a SOIC-16 integrated circuit package, named R3-WORK. The body is a black worn semiconductor chip with engraved text “R3-WORK SOIC-16”. Multiple articulated metallic legs made from bent IC leads. One arm is a soldering iron tool, the other a mechanical repair claw. Exposed colored wires under a torn cloth cape. Retro industrial cyberpunk aesthetic. Soft cinematic lighting. High detail mechanical textures. Clean white background. Front orthographic render. PS1/N64 inspired proportions but modern render quality.
```
